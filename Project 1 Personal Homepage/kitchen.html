🌶️ Mapo Tofu 麻婆豆腐

🌶️🌶️🌶️

⏱️ 25 minutes · 👩‍🍳 Easy · Sichuan

Silky tofu swimming in a numbing, spicy sauce with crumbled pork. The classic Sichuan dish: bright, oily and completely addictive over rice.

🥬 Ingredients

  • 1 block soft tofu, cut into cubes
  • 150 g ground pork or beef
  • 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, freshly ground
  • Garlic, ginger, scallions

📋 Steps

  1. Brown the meat in oil until crisp at the edges.
  2. Add doubanjiang, garlic and ginger; fry until the oil glows red.
  3. Slide in the tofu with stock, simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Thicken with a cornstarch slurry, top with peppercorn and scallions.

🍗 Chongqing Chicken 辣子鸡

🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️

⏱️ 45 minutes · 👩‍🍳 Medium · Chongqing

Crunchy fried chicken bites hidden in a mountain of dried red chilies. The joy is fishing for chicken pieces with your chopsticks — the chilies aren't there to be eaten, they're the perfume.

🥬 Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken thigh, cubed
  • A big handful of dried red chilies
  • 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • Garlic, ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine
  • Cornstarch for a crispy coat

📋 Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with wine, soy sauce and cornstarch.
  2. Deep-fry until deep golden and crunchy.
  3. Stir-fry chilies and peppercorns until fragrant, not burnt.
  4. Toss the chicken back in with garlic and scallions.

🍜 Hot & Sour Noodles 酸辣粉

🌶️🌶️🌶️

⏱️ 20 minutes · 👩‍🍳 Easy · Chongqing

Slippery sweet-potato noodles in a broth that hits sour, spicy and savory all at once. My favorite late-night bowl after a long day of coding.

🥬 Ingredients

  • Sweet-potato glass noodles
  • Chinese black vinegar and light soy sauce
  • Chili oil with sesame seeds
  • Garlic paste, peanuts, cilantro, scallions

📋 Steps

  1. Soak and boil the noodles until just tender.
  2. Build the sauce in a bowl: vinegar, soy, chili oil, garlic.
  3. Pour hot broth over the sauce to bloom it.
  4. Add noodles, then crown with peanuts and cilantro.

🍲 Dan Dan Noodles 担担面

🌶️🌶️🌶️

⏱️ 30 minutes · 👩‍🍳 Easy · Sichuan

Named after the poles street vendors used to carry the noodles. A little sauce at the bottom of the bowl, a nest of noodles on top, and crispy minced pork to finish.

🥬 Ingredients

  • Thin wheat noodles
  • Ground pork, sui mi ya cai (preserved mustard)
  • Chili oil, sesame paste, black vinegar
  • Garlic, Sichuan peppercorn, scallions

📋 Steps

  1. Crisp the pork with the preserved mustard.
  2. Whisk the sauce: sesame paste, chili oil, soy, vinegar, garlic.
  3. Boil the noodles until just past al dente.
  4. Sauce first, noodles on top, pork to crown.
